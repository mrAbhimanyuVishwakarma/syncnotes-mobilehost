## How to Sync & Manage Notes

To manage all your notes, click the **☰ (Menu)** button on any floating sticky note to open the **SyncNotes Dashboard**. From there, you have three powerful options:

### 1. 📱 Scan to Sync (Direct Mobile Transfer)
Want to send notes from your phone to your PC instantly without creating an account?
1. Open the SyncNotes Dashboard and click **Scan to Sync**.
2. A secure, temporary QR Code will appear on your screen.
3. Open your phone's camera and scan the QR Code.
4. Your phone will open a secure webpage that connects directly to your PC.
5. On your phone, select your exported JSON notes file. It will instantly beam over the internet directly into your Chrome Extension!

### 2. ⬇️ Import File (Manual Restore)
If you have a backup of your notes, or exported them manually from another device:
1. Click the **Import File** button.
2. Select your `.json` or `.txt` file containing your notes.
3. The notes will be instantly added to your dashboard!

### 3. ⬆️ Export All (JSON Backup)
Always keep your data safe!
1. Click the **Export All (JSON)** button.
2. The extension will generate a single file containing every note you've ever taken and download it to your computer.
